# BLACK MAY 250 — Modular Orbital Vehicle

A 125 ft orbital vehicle whose primary structure is designed to be **3D printed in 10 ft segments** and assembled with bolted shear-lock flange joints — built for in-orbit assembly, repair, and module replacement.

## Vehicle layout (125 ft overall, 22 ft tall)

| Section | Stations | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Forward pressurized | 0–50 ft | Crew cabin, EVA airlock modules, docking ring, avionics bay |
| Cargo bay truss | 50–95 ft | 45 ft bay, 24 ft internal width, robotic rail system |
| Transition | 95–105 ft | Truss-to-bulkhead load transfer, 4 diagonal compression beams |
| Thrust bulkhead | 105–115 ft | 26 ft OD ring, printed as 2 half-rings |
| Engine structure | 110–125 ft | Replaceable engine cassettes, ±7° thrust vectoring |

## Key ratings

- **Keel / box beam:** 48×60 in rectangular box beam, printed lattice core (18–22% fill), 35M ft-lb bending design load, >8,000,000 lb compression
- **Flange joints:** shear-lock interfaces, 1–1.5 in Inconel bolts, 28–48 bolts per 10 ft module, 2,200 ft-lb torque, **2.5M lb shear per interface**
- **Thrust bulkhead:** rated **4,000,000 lb combined thrust**
- **Cargo bay:** satellite capture shock load rating 150,000 lb impulse; 45° cross-braced truss cage
- **Sensor gimbal:** 48 in diameter, 3,000–5,000 lb payload rating, elastomer-isolated
- **Solar mounts:** 4 dorsal nodes @ 12,000 lb each, 40 ft panel span per wing, 80–120 kW target
- **Bay doors:** 45 ft, 25,000 lb actuators
- **Pressure barrel:** 26 ft external / 24 ft internal, dual O-ring slip-fit collars, ring frames @ 36 in, 6 longerons
