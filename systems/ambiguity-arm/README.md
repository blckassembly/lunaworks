# AMBIGUITY™-ARM — Large-Format Additive Manufacturing Cell

A 6-axis structural deposition system for printing **15–25 ft UAV airframes** in carbon-fiber PEEK/PEKK with continuous fiber reinforcement — 8–12 hours per frame. The manufacturing engine of the Lunaworks portfolio: the machine that prints the structures.

## CORE — 6-axis deposition head (900–1,100 lb, 60–66 in tall)

- **CORE-01** Carriage mount interface plate — 28–32 in OD, 16× M20 bolts, flatness ≤ 0.02 mm
- **CORE-02** A-axis pitch drive — crossed-roller bearing, ≥ 4,000 ft-lb dynamic torque, 15–20 kW
- **CORE-03** B-axis yaw drive — harmonic drive, ≥ 2,500 Nm, zero backlash
- **CORE-04** C-axis continuous roll — fiber alignment rotation, hollow bore for fiber routing
- **CORE-05** High-temperature pellet extruder — CF-PEEK/PEKK, 450°C max, 20–30 kW, 4–6 heater zones
- **CORE-06** Continuous fiber injection collar — 4–8 strands into the melt stream, inline tension sensing
- **CORE-07** Co-extrusion nozzle — 20 mm bead width, 4 mm layer height, replaceable tip

## ARM — Z-column + carriage (~8,000–9,000 lb)

- 14 ft column modules stack to **42 ft**; 36 ft linear rails at **±0.25 mm repeatability**
- Rack & pinion drive, 25 kW Z-servo, 35 ft usable travel, 48–60 in nozzle reach

## GANTRY — overhead platform

- **80 ft span** truss on 40 ft towers; dual X-axis rails at ±0.5 mm position accuracy
- Cell: 80 ft wide, 48–50 ft clearance, **4–8 deposition heads per cell**
