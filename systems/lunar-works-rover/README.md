# Lunar Works — Autonomous Surface Mobility Platform

A bidirectional lunar infrastructure rover: identical forward and rear geometry on a dual-face structural beam, so it never has to turn around.

- **Full-width sensor spine** — flush horizontal sensing array, redundant front/rear, no protrusions
- **Graphite titanium X-brace frame** — primary load path, side-impact reinforcement
- **Retractable sensor mast** on a modular rotation collar
- **Non-pneumatic lattice wheels** — open composite structure, dust-sealed hub motors, independent articulated suspension
- **Materials:** Nassau White Ceramic Composite body, brushed graphite titanium, bead-blasted aluminium, regolith-gray composite wheels
- **Size:** ~3.8 m long × 2.0 m wide
