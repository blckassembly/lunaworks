# LUNAWORKS

**An aerospace design portfolio by Chip Wongsang.**

Lunaworks is a body of mechanical and aerospace engineering design work — 5,000+ research and design files — built on one doctrine: **systems are modules, interfaces are standardized, and everything is designed to be manufactured, serviced, and replaced.** Additive manufacturing is the default production assumption, not an afterthought.

## Design doctrine

1. **Modular by default** — vehicles, suits, satellites, and buildings all decompose into replaceable modules on standardized interfaces.
2. **Additive first** — parts are designed for 3D printing (lattice cores, printed segments, DMLS/SLS shells) with DFM/DFA notes at the drawing level.
3. **Failure stays local** — distributed systems, independent zones, no single-point failures.
4. **Serviceable in the field** — bolted, flanged, and cassette-based joints over welded monoliths.
